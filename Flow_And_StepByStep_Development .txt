Step 9: Add Saga Pattern

Use Order Service as Saga Orchestrator.

Successful Flow
1. Order Service creates order as Pending
2. Order Service publishes OrderCreatedEvent
3. Inventory Service reserves stock
4. Inventory Service publishes StockReservedEvent
5. Payment Service processes payment
6. Payment Service publishes PaymentCompletedEvent
7. Order Service marks order as Confirmed
8. Inventory Service confirms stock deduction
9. Notification Service sends confirmation
Failed Stock Flow
1. Order Service creates order as Pending
2. Inventory Service checks stock
3. Stock not available
4. Inventory Service publishes StockRejectedEvent
5. Order Service marks order as Cancelled
6. Notification Service sends failure notification
Failed Payment Flow
1. Order Service creates order as Pending
2. Inventory Service reserves stock
3. Payment Service fails payment
4. Payment Service publishes PaymentFailedEvent
5. Order Service marks order as Failed
6. Inventory Service releases stock
7. Notification Service sends payment failed message

This is your Distributed Transaction practical implementation.
